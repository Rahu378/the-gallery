Reading the colours

Cyan means a Gemini decision is live. Orange marks the cut itself — the shot on air. Amber means the system is running deterministic: the model timed out, hit quota, or has no credentials. Green is healthy state and DRS range.

A deterministic cut is never shown in cyan. If the colour says Gemini chose, Gemini chose.

Operator controls

Click a driver in the timing tower or a contested position to track them — the map rings that car, dims the rest of the field, and the decision log filters to entries mentioning them. Click again or press Esc to clear.

Hovering the decision log freezes it so an entry can be read without the list reordering.
